They are illustrative serving references, not photographs of the supplied recipes or evidence of their nutrition. Existing ingredients, quantities and nutrition remain the source of truth. CSS displays individual atlas servings without modifying the source image. Both files are bundled for offline use. Recipe-detail captions disclose their illustrative purpose; thumbnails are decorative beside the recipe name, and larger references have descriptive accessible labels.

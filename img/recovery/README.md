# Recovery exercise sketches

Seventeen original movement illustrations, each with a start and finish position, use simple black outlines on white to match the exercise library sketch style. They replace the procedural capsule figures. No photographs or shaded anatomical renders are shipped in this set.

Each movement was generated and reviewed separately. The two positions have their own portrait SVG viewports over an embedded WebP board. Existing URLs and exercise identifiers remain unchanged. `generated-20261024.json` records the source PNG hashes; originals were retained under `/workspace/generated_images` during generation. `tools/export_generated_art.py` only encodes and packages the reviewed artwork. The legacy procedural generator is retired.

Illustrations support the written cues; they do not change programme prescriptions. Existing instructions, sets, repetitions, hold times and saved routines remain authoritative.
